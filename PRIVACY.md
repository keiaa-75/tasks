# Privacy Policy

This application does not collect, store, or share any personal information, data, or usage statistics from its users.

All data accessed by the application is done so with your explicit permission through Google's OAuth consent screen. The application's access is strictly limited to the Google Tasks API for the sole purpose of displaying your tasks.

This application does not use any third-party analytics or advertising frameworks.

Your data is your own. This application is simply a client that interacts with the Google Tasks API on your behalf.
